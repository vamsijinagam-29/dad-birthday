MY FIRST HERO

NANNA

1 August 1981

A father who never searched for comfort... but worked every single day to give comfort to his family.

His Journey

Hard Work

Every morning he travelled to Rajam, worked in Munna Tailors, returned home, and continued stitching late into the night.

Sacrifice

He had opportunities to stay in town for work, but he chose to stay with us, because seeing his children return from school meant more than anything else.

Love

Whatever we needed, he somehow managed to provide, even during the most difficult days.

My Inspiration

He taught me patience, responsibility, respect, kindness, and what it truly means to love a family.

Beautiful Memories ❤️

Every picture holds a memory... Every memory holds a piece of my heart.

A Letter To My Father ❤️

Dear Nanna,

Thank you... Thank you for every sleepless night, every sacrifice, every smile, every silent struggle that you never showed us.

You never asked life for luxury. You only wished that your family should never feel any difficulty.

Every stitch you made as a tailor carried our dreams. Every day you worked became another step towards our future.

Whenever we needed something, somehow... you always found a way.

Today, whatever I am, and whatever I become in the future, will always be because of you.

You are not only my father. You are my teacher, my protector, my guide, and my greatest inspiration.

I will always be proud to call myself Your Son.

My Promise To You ❤️

Nanna...

Just 2½ more years...

I will complete my studies.

I will become a source of income for our family.

I will take responsibility for our home.

I will support you, just like you supported me every single day of my life.

I don't want you to work forever.

One day, I want you to relax, smile, and finally enjoy life, because then... it will be my turn.

I Promise You ❤️
